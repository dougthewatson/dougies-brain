# Project: Hot Sauce Small-Batch Gift Run

Time-bounded project. Goal: produce 12 labeled 150mL bottles of fermented hot sauce to give as gifts by end of June 2026.

---

## Status

In progress. Batch B-24 (jalapeño base) is in active fermentation. Labeling design not started.

## Deliverables

- [ ] Ferment and taste-test batch B-24 (June 2)
- [ ] Blend and strain to target consistency
- [ ] Source 150mL woozy bottles (12 units)
- [ ] Design and print labels (simple kraft stock, hand-stamp approach)
- [ ] Fill, cap, and label 12 bottles
- [ ] Deliver by June 28

## Recipe (B-24 baseline)

- 500g fresh jalapeños, stems removed
- 100g white onion
- 4 cloves garlic
- 27g non-iodized sea salt (3% of total weight: ~615g vegetables + ~300g brine water)
- Brine: ~300mL filtered water to submerge

Ferment 3-4 weeks at 18-20°C. Blend with 60mL apple cider vinegar post-ferment. Adjust salt and acid to taste.

## Notes

- Previous jalapeño batch (B-19) was too thin, so strain more aggressively this time.
- Label design: keep it minimal. Batch number, date, heat level. No need to impress; this is a gift.
