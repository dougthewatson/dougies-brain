## Running observations

- Room temp above 22°C shortens ferment time by 30-40%. Adjust tasting window forward.
- Jalapeño batches need a weight to keep caps submerged; they float aggressively.
- Ginger bug starter kept in fridge; feed once a week when dormant.

## Reference brine ratios

| Use case | Salt % by weight |
|---|---|
| Soft vegetables (cucumber, squash) | 2.0% |
| Dense vegetables (cabbage, carrot) | 2.5% |
| Hot peppers / hot sauce | 3.0% |
| Summer batches (above 22°C ambient) | add +0.5% |
